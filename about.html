Her Story

Makeup as a craft, not a performance

Arzoo Gupta has spent 7+ years behind the brush, working with brides across Gurgaon, Dubai and beyond who want to look like the most polished version of themselves — never a stranger in the mirror. From destination weddings to intimate engagements, her training in HD and airbrush technique means every look is built to survive a long wedding day, camera flashes, and the odd happy tear.

What sets her apart is patience: every booking starts with a real conversation about the outfit, the venue lighting, and how the bride actually wants to feel — confident, radiant, still recognisably herself. She travels globally for weddings and shoots, carrying that same care wherever the destination.

Philosophy

Three things every bride can expect

01

Skin‑first application

Prep and products are chosen for your skin, not a one‑size finish — so it looks like skin, not a mask, in every photo.

02

Built to last

HD and airbrush formulas that hold through sangeet dancing, monsoon humidity, and every emotional moment.

03

Led by the bride

Your trial sets the direction. Nothing is applied on the wedding day that wasn't agreed on beforehand.
